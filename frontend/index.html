    RPC Sentinel — a GenLayer Intelligent Contract demo. It answers one narrow
    question: whether the endpoint currently behaves consistently with the expected
    chain and basic JSON-RPC integrity requirements.
    

      It is not an endorsement that a provider is honest or malicious, not a safety or
      availability guarantee, not a claim that an endpoint is canonical, and not a
      network scanner. History is the app's own on-chain record, not an independent
      archival oracle. Validity is limited to the exact probe defined in the
      contract's public configuration.
